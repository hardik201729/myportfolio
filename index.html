What was asked

“Make the counsellors call faster.”

What was underneath

Order, not speed. leads a month were worked first-in, first-out, so ready-to-book students went cold in the queue.

What I did

  • Compared 6 months of won and lost leads to find intent signals.
  • Rule-based score first, logistic regression second.
  • Hot leads auto-routed to a counsellor, with a WhatsApp first touch.
  • Speed-to-lead: 10 minutes. Booking conversion +44%.

My role: discovery with the sales floor, scoring logic, routing rules and rollout with engineering and sales ops.

What was asked

What was underneath

Volume wasn't the bottleneck. Getting a human to read the application was.

What I did

  • Pulls jobs daily from LinkedIn, Wellfound and job-alert emails, then removes duplicates.
  • Scores fit: title 30%, skills 40%, experience 15%, text similarity 15%.
  • Finds 2 recruiters per job and referral contacts, on a fixed credit budget.
  • Sends tailored emails and two follow-ups, and stops the moment someone replies.

How I work

Four rules.

Each one came from a mistake I made once. Each card below is interactive.

01

Fix the drop-off, not the feature request.

Every request arrives as a solution. Before writing a PRD, I find the step where people leave and ask what they were trying to do when they left.

What was asked“Add a referral banner on Home.”
What was meantNew users don't trust an app they found five minutes ago.
hover or tap the card
02

No AI answer ships without an eval.

A demo proves an AI can be right. An eval set proves how often it's wrong. I write the test questions before the prompt, and the guardrails before the launch.

14% wrong answers
03

One metric per bet.

Ten metrics on a dashboard means nobody owns any of them. Each bet gets one number we agree on before building, and a date we check it.

page viewsapp openstime on screennotifications sentdownloadspayment successMAUclickssessions
04

Ship small. Let the test decide.

Opinions are cheap and launches are expensive. I'd rather ship half a feature to 10% of users on Monday than the whole thing to everyone next month.
