The short version

Speed wasn't the real problem; order was. I scored every lead on intent signals, routed the hottest to a counsellor within minutes and started a WhatsApp first touch. Same team, same leads, 44% more bookings.

01

Context

University Living sells student accommodation abroad. Leads come from the website, partners and campaigns, about a month. A team of counsellors calls each one, answers questions and helps them book.

Leadership saw slow follow-up and asked for the obvious fix: make counsellors call faster.

02

The problem behind the problem

I sat with the sales floor for a week and pulled 6 months of won and lost leads. Counsellors were working hard. They were just working the queue top to bottom, so a student flying out in two weeks waited behind someone browsing for next year.

Calling everyone faster was impossible. Calling the right people first was not.
03

The scoring model

Version 1 was a rule-based score we could explain to the sales floor in one slide. Once it was trusted, version 2 swapped in a logistic regression trained on the same 6 months of outcomes.

Routing, not just scoring

What I learned

  • Ship the explainable version first. Counsellors trusted rules they understood, which earned the right to ship the model.
  • The workflow is the product. Scoring without routing would have been a dashboard nobody opened.
